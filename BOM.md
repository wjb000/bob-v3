# BOM

November 2026 planning prices, before shipping and tax. On top of Bob v2. A second arm is not in the total.

The servo price is the AlohaMini2 STS3215 line, about $15.97, not a cheaper no-name servo. Screw and rod prices are the old estimates, now for **700 mm** instead of 500 mm. Check the listing.

About 140 M3 screws and 120 M3 nuts. The traps are sized for a 5.5 mm nut, 2.4 mm thick. No heat-set inserts.

The deck is 480 × 340 mm, six tiles, so the SO-101 reaches past the nose. The solid mesh is 3.20 kg, so the filament line is $58 and the total is $230. Printed with 15% gyroid the spool use is lower. See `cad/reports/manifest.json`.

From a bare bench, add a Pi 5 (about $93 for a 2 GB board), a used hoverboard (about $60), an ESP32 (about $8), and one USB camera (about $17). 230 + 93 + 60 + 8 + 17 is about $410.

## Not in the $230

| Upgrade | When |

| Second SO-101, about $110 | After one hand fails a job. |
| 608ZZ in the top cap | If the printed 8.6 mm bushing wears. |
| Wrist camera | The v2 cameras are the eyes. |
| Bought plate casters | If the 30 mm rollers catch on rugs. |
| Longer `ROD_LEN` | Only if 700 mm is still short. |

Metal that is not printed: screws, nuts, the two rods, the leadscrew, the LM8UU, the motors, and the hoverboard hubs. A 608ZZ is optional and not required to assemble.
